System Purpose

Decision-support, not diagnosis

This system is designed to demonstrate how several pre-trained and trained models can be coordinated to estimate behavioural context. It should be interpreted as an explainable decision-support prototype for behavioural-state analysis, not as a medical or psychological diagnosis.
