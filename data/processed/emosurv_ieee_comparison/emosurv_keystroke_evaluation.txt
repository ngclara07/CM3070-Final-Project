METHODOLOGICAL STATUS
--------------------
The EmoSurv datasets contain emotion labels rather than SenseFuzeAI behavioural-state labels.
Focused, distracted, fatigued and overloaded are therefore weakly supervised proxy labels.

Proxy mapping:
  Calm   -> focused
  Angry  -> overloaded
  Sad    -> fatigued
  Neutral / Happy -> focused or distracted according to typing-dynamics irregularity

Distraction threshold: 0.621128
