Methodological Note
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The pretrained CLIP ViT-L/14 model is used as a frozen visual feature extractor. The calibration process retrains only the downstream behavioural-state classifier.

Evaluation is grouped by source video to prevent frames originating from the same video appearing in both training and test sets. This reduces frame-level leakage and produces a more defensible estimate of live webcam generalisation.
