Leakage / robustness validation notes
=====================================

Method:
- Permutation testing uses only the development partition.
- The untouched held-out test partition is not used.
- Group-aware CV boundaries are retained.

Interpretation:
- For a reasonably balanced four-class task, chance-like macro-F1 is expected to be approximately 0.25.
- Permutation performance close to chance is desirable.
- Persistently high permutation performance can indicate leakage or label-coded artifacts.
- Very high text-only performance may indicate that textual prompts encode the behavioural label rather than natural behaviour.
